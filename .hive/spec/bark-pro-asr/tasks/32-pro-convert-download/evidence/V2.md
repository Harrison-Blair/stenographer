- Full lock (manager decision 4): `src/stenographer/assets/convert/constraints.txt`.

    no hashes.
  - pip applies it with `-c` on both installs.
  - Its sha256 is in `BARK_PRO_PINS.constraints_sha256`. The download checks
    that sha256 before pip runs.
  - Tests check that the lock matches its sha256, that every line is an exact
    `==` with no hash or URL, and that the lock agrees with every top-level pin.
