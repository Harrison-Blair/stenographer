# V4 — dictation VAD before the decode (req 4)

`lib/transcribe/dictation_vad.py`:
- `dictation_speech(samples, vad_model_path)` runs `PhraseSegmenter("mic", path)`
  (the live class: same `silero_vad.onnx`, threshold 0.5, min speech 0.25 s,
  min silence 0.6 s, max 25 s) over the mono capture, accept + flush.
- `keep_speech` joins each region with up to 0.25 s of the audio before it; a
  pre-pad stops at the capture start and at the previous region's end.
- `UtterancePipeline(speech_filter=…)` runs it after the RMS gate. An empty
  buffer → no `worker.transcribe` call, empty `TranscriptionResult`, outcome
  `SILENT`, `failure=empty` as for any empty transcript. Otherwise only the
  kept speech goes to the worker; `vad_seconds` is the kept length (Model
  reports its whole input).
- `Daemon.build` passes the real filter with
  `<data_dir>/models/live/silero_vad.onnx`, resolved per utterance.
