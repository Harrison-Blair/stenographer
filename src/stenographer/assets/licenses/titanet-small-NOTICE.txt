TitaNet small attribution

stenographer uses the NVIDIA NeMo TitaNet small speaker-embedding model
(`nemo_en_titanet_small.onnx`) for live and Files speaker labels. stenographer
does not ship the model file. `stenographer model download --live` fetches it
at a pinned sha256 from the sherpa-onnx release assets (an ONNX export of the
NeMo checkpoint):
https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/download/speaker-recongition-models/nemo_en_titanet_small.onnx

Model: titanet_small, by NVIDIA.
https://catalog.ngc.nvidia.com/orgs/nvidia/teams/nemo/models/titanet_small

Paper: TitaNet: Neural Model for speaker representation with 1D Depth-wise
separable convolutions and global context. arXiv:2110.04410.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.04410

License: the model card says "License to use this model is covered by the
license of the NeMo Toolkit." The NeMo Toolkit is licensed under the Apache
License, Version 2.0.
https://github.com/NVIDIA/NeMo/blob/main/LICENSE
https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
