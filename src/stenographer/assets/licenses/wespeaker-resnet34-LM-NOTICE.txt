WeSpeaker ResNet34-LM attribution

stenographer ships the WeSpeaker ResNet34-LM speaker-embedding model, trained
on VoxCeleb, as `assets/models/wespeaker_en_voxceleb_resnet34_LM_mean.onnx`
(sha256 280d78ccc3a8a3ffdf4d9ef867e16faa68d2beb107ad1de7a844285d3522fcd6) and
uses it for live and Files speaker labels.

Modification: mean subtraction added to the ONNX graph by stenographer. The
two added nodes subtract the mean over time of the input features, which the
model expects and sherpa-onnx does not do. Nothing else in the model changed.
The script `scripts/speaker_model_mean.py` in the stenographer source makes the
file from the sherpa-onnx ONNX export:
https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/download/speaker-recongition-models/wespeaker_en_voxceleb_resnet34_LM.onnx
sha256 e9848563da86f263117134dfd7ad63c92355b37de492b55e325400c9d9c39012

Model: voxceleb_resnet34_LM, by the WeSpeaker team (wenet-e2e).
https://github.com/wenet-e2e/wespeaker
https://github.com/wenet-e2e/wespeaker/blob/master/docs/pretrained.md

Paper: Wespeaker: A Research and Production oriented Speaker Embedding Learning
Toolkit. arXiv:2210.17016.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.17016
